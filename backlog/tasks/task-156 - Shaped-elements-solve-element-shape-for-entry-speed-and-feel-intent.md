## Engine diagnosis and remaining work

2026-11-19 force decomposition of the closest measured hybrid 4-car, 14 m/s candidate (pull 3.0 g, crest −1.10 g, flank 20 degrees, taper 0.05 rad) located the raw longitudinal event near local distance 11.20 m. At that point rail curvature was 0.09531 m⁻¹ (10.49 m radius), pitch was −18.23 degrees, and longitudinal force was −5.647 g: heart-curvature projection −5.254 g, train-acceleration projection −0.081 g, and gravity −0.313 g. The force-filtered value at the same point was −0.349 g. The filtered minimum was delayed to local distance 12.65 m, where it was −3.021 g while raw force had recovered to −1.448 g. The finite-difference heart-curvature projection converged at the normal 0.02 m step: −0.2700, −0.2696, and −0.2695 m⁻¹ at 0.08, 0.04, and 0.02 m steps. This points to a real curvature-gradient event from the offset heartline as rail curvature falls from about 0.095 m⁻¹ to nearly zero over roughly 1.1 m; it does not support a finite-difference sign or small-step artifact.

A bounded test-only experiment limited the curvature-gradient term to 0.75 g at 14 m/s, equivalent to a rail-curvature slope cap of 0.03128 m⁻². With the existing crest-target and flank calibration updates over three actual-train previews, longitudinal maxima stayed at or below 1.293 g. The three candidates delivered −0.734, −0.962, and −1.305 g, with headroom −0.570, −0.725, and −0.876 g. Their heights increased from 2.309 to 3.200 to 4.186 m, while vertical minima worsened from −2.070 to −2.225 to −2.376 g. None passed the target and force-margin requirements. The limiter prototype and all temporary diagnostic code were removed; the production solver does not use that limiter.

The vertical failure is independent of the longitudinal curvature-gradient diagnosis. The earlier shallow, radius-valid hybrid candidate at 1.670 m height also had per-car vertical minima between −2.54 and −2.65 g and longitudinal maxima between 2.51 and 2.69 g, so reducing height alone has not been shown to solve the envelope. No hybrid coefficient rows passed the 588-candidate study, and the production coefficient table has not been generated or integrated. The current production solver still refuses the exact hyper 34 m/s hill and its −0.95 g floater hash/save smoke.

Follow-up should first map each car's vertical force minimum to local speed, profile curvature, local height, and the train's mean height at that same step. Then shape and calibrate vertical curvature and longitudinal curvature rate together against the actual train preview, keeping the existing limits and margins. Do not generate or ship coefficient rows until representative hill cases pass actual-train force, airtime-duration, and minimum-radius checks. Once feasible seed cells are measured for both physics configurations, validate interpolation coverage before rerunning the 14–36 m/s model/train matrix and the cold/warm WASM p95 benchmark.
